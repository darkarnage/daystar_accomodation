About CampusNest

CampusNest helps Daystar University students at the Athi River campus find safe, affordable places to live, and helps nearby landlords reach students directly.

For students

Search by rent, room type, amenities and distance to campus. Create a free account to save favourites and see landlord phone numbers and emails.

For landlords

Create a landlord account and submit your property with photos. An admin checks each listing before it appears. Edited listings are checked again.

Safety tips

  • Always visit the place before paying anything.
  • Go with a friend and ask for a written agreement.
  • Never pay a deposit to someone you have not met.
