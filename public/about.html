Student housing, made easier to find

CampusNest helps students find safe, affordable places to live near Daystar University, Athi River, and helps property owners reach students directly.

Browse accommodation

For students

Search by rent, room type, amenities and distance to campus. Browsing is open to everyone. Create a free account to save favourites and see the owner's phone number and email.

Create a student account

For property owners

No account needed. Use the listing form to submit your property with photos and your contact details. An admin checks each listing before it appears, and edited listings are checked again.

List a property

How we check listings

Every submission starts as pending. An admin reviews the details and either approves it or rejects it with a reason. Only approved listings that are still available appear on CampusNest.
